# Hackathon Projects

These are the three final projects submitted at the USF × Ripple XRPL Hackathon.

---

## **Shahid** — Archive for Political Violence

An evidence preservation platform designed to protect and archive records on behalf of victims of political violence. Submissions are manually reviewed and anchored on the XRP Ledger for tamper-proof long-term storage.

[View project →](./shahid/)

---

## **EviAir** — Citizen Air Quality Reporting

An open-source citizen air quality reporting platform that anchors pollution readings as tamper-evident evidence on the XRP Ledger. Readings are cross-validated via consensus and queryable by XRPL transaction ID.

[View project →](./eviair/)

---

## **MedVerify** — Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Tracker

A decentralized pharmaceutical supply chain tracker built on the XRP Ledger. Every custody handoff—from factory floor to dispensing counter—is anchored on-chain as a tamper-proof record. Patients scan a QR code to see the full verified journey of their medication with AI-generated summaries and anomaly detection.

[View project →](./medverify/)
