USF x Ripple Hackathon

Student Projects

Three projects built on the XRP Ledger during the hackathon, tackling real-world social good challenges.

Human Rights Shahid

An evidence preservation platform for victims of political violence. Submissions are manually reviewed and anchored on the XRP Ledger for tamper-proof long-term storage.

GitHub ↗
Environment EviAir

An open citizen air quality reporting platform that anchors pollution readings as tamper-evident evidence on the XRP Ledger, cross-validated via consensus and queryable by transaction ID.

GitHub ↗
Healthcare MedVerify

A decentralized pharmaceutical supply chain tracker. Every custody handoff from factory to dispensing counter is anchored on-chain. Patients scan a QR code to see the full verified journey of their medication.
