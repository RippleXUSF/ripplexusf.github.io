# XRPL Docs

A reference guide to XRPL concepts and fundamentals for hackathon participants.

**Start here:** Read chapter 01 before diving into the rest.

## Table of contents

1. **[Ripple vs. XRPL](./01-ripple-vs-xrpl.md)** — Understand the distinction between Ripple (the company), the XRP Ledger (the protocol), and XRP (the asset).

2. **[Accounts & Reserves](./02-accounts-and-reserves.md)** — Learn how accounts exist on the ledger, and why the reserve requirement is critical for your projects.

3. **[Transactions](./03-transactions.md)** — Explore the lifecycle of every action on the ledger: build, sign, submit, and validate.

4. **[Consensus](./04-consensus.md)** — Discover how the XRP Ledger achieves agreement without proof-of-work or proof-of-stake.

5. **[Trust Lines](./05-trust-lines.md)** — Master the primitive that makes issued currencies work, and learn why they differ from smart contracts.

6. **[DEX & AMM](./06-dex-and-amm.md)** — Place orders and provide liquidity using the ledger's native decentralized exchange and automated market maker.

7. **[Escrow, Channels & NFTs](./07-escrow-channels-nfts.md)** — Use three powerful native primitives: time-locked escrow, payment channels, and non-fungible tokens.

8. **[First Transaction (hands-on)](./08-first-transaction.md)** — End-to-end walkthrough: install the SDK, fund a testnet wallet, send XRP, and verify in the explorer.

9. **[Glossary](./glossary.md)** — Quick reference for terms used throughout these docs and the official XRPL specification.

10. **[Resources](./resources.md)** — Curated links to official tutorials, dev tools, community channels, and sample code.
